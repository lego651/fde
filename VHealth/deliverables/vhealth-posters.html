V-Health Supermarket Posters

10 A4 designs for supermarket community boards · Prepared Nov 12, 2026

Each poster leads with a different reason to visit. All of them repeat the same core facts: direct billing, benefits that reset Dec 31, massage, osteopathy and acupuncture, and an experienced team. Every poster has its own QR code and tracking code. The booking data will show which one works, so you don't have to guess.

How to tell which poster works

StepWhat to do
1. Choose 3Start with V1 (benefits expire), V2 (direct billing) and V4 (first-visit offer). They fit the December deadline best.
2. One per storePut up a different version at each supermarket (e.g. V1 at store A, V2 at store B, V4 at store C). Write down the store and the date it went up.
3. Swap after 2 weeksRotate the versions between stores, so a busy store doesn't make one poster look better than it is.
4. CountEach QR code opens the booking page with utm_campaign=poster-vN (shows up in GA4). Phone callers are asked "How did you hear about us?" and given the code on the poster (P1–P10). The AI engineer adds a "Poster" option to the booking-form dropdown.
5. DecideAfter 4 weeks, keep the poster with the most new clients per store-week. Replace the rest with new versions. If posters bring fewer than 2 new clients a month in total, stop printing.

Before printing

  • Ask the store first. Most supermarket boards allow community notices up to letter or A4 size. Some ask for a date stamp. Some don't allow businesses at all.
  • Discount (V4): if a client uses insurance, bill the insurer the discounted price, not the full price. Billing the full amount while giving a discount can break insurer rules.
  • Health claims: the wording says "may help" and makes no promises. RMT and acupuncture regulators have advertising standards. Check V5 and V6 against them before printing.
  • No "Dr." on any poster. V6 says "medically trained" and lists registered titles only.
  • Before printing, confirm: the insurer list, the hours (Mon–Fri 9:30–8:30, Sat–Sun 9:30–6) and the prices (30 min $55 · 45 min $85 · 60 min $110).
  • How to print: open the local file /Users/jason/TEMP/vhealth-posters.html in Chrome → Print → Paper A4, Margins None, turn on Background graphics. You get one poster per page. Scan each QR code with your phone before putting it up.
