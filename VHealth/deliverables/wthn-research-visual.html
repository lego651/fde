not its discounts.

WTHN made acupuncture easy for first-timers. Its discounts work because it doesn't bill insurers. V-Health direct-bills about 95% of visits, so for V-Health the discounts become a billing and delisting risk.

2The one difference that matters: who pays

WTHN client pays

A discount = the client pays less. No insurer is involved.13

V-Health insurer pays ~95%

A discount = the insurer pays less, so the client barely notices. The claim must show the lower price. Getting it wrong can get the clinic removed from the insurer's list.14,15
