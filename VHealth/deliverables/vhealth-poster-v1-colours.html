V-Health Benefits Poster V1 Colours

Revision 3 · A4 supermarket poster · Nov 12, 2026

The benefits-card poster (D) now leads with "Use it or lose it.", with one short line underneath so people know it's about insurance. It comes in 9 colour schemes chosen for Calgary and Canadian tastes. The classic poster (A) is kept, shown in its original colours and in the same 9 schemes so you can compare them side by side.

  • My pick: D1 (clinic white + teal). It matches your logo, looks like a healthcare provider, and a light poster stands out on a supermarket board. For a second store, D2 (Canadian red) or D5 (evergreen, for the holiday season).
  • Test two at once: put D1 in one store and D2 or D5 in another. Each poster has its own QR tag (V1D1, V1D2…), so GA4 shows which colour scheme brings more bookings.
  • QR codes open https://app.vhealth.ca/booking, tested and working. Scan once before printing.
  • How to print: open /Users/jason/TEMP/vhealth-poster-v1-colours.html in Chrome → Print → A4, Margins None, turn on Background graphics.
