Can Kids Map or Roam sell a $4.99/month plan?

Nov 8, 2026 · Builds on Future vision, Monetization plan, Alpha/beta revenue (Coffee Pass) and Live reports + Coins. [n] = source at the bottom. ESTIMATE = my own number.

Short answer: yes, but only for 3 kinds of things. People don't pay for "a better map". Across every paid app I checked, people keep paying each month for one of these:
  1. It saves more money than it costs. Entertainment® sells 2-for-1 local deals at $4.99/month or $34.99/year in Canada 4. Edmonton's CoffeePass sells for $42.75 9. → Roam fits this best: the Coffee Pass becomes a Roam Pass you renew each season.
  2. It keeps your family's memories or your family safe. Tinybeans has ~95,000 parents paying US$74.99/year, and 89–91% renew 5. Life360 has 2.8 million paying families at ~US$137/year 1. → Kids Map fits this: a Family+ plan built around each kid's yearbook (passport stamps + Moments), not around the map.
  3. It's a tool for something you do every week. AllTrails Plus US$35.99/yr, Peak US$79.99/yr 2. Strava US$79.99/yr 6. → This only works with a big user base, so it comes later.
My pick: ① Roam Pass first (works with few users, cafes pay the cost). ② Kids Map Family+ second, tested with a "fake door" button before building. ③ Keep everything that's free today free.
Honest math: free apps usually convert only ~2.1–2.2% of users to paid 3. At 5,000 monthly parents that's ~100 payers ≈ $500/month. A subscription won't be the main money for a while. The Pass can be, because each sale is bigger and doesn't need a huge crowd.

5. Things to watch

  • Dudu's cost can eat the fee. One chat ≈ $0.03. A heavy user doing 5 chats a day = ~$4.50/month, almost the whole $4.99. Keep a fair-use cap (e.g. 300 chats/month) even for paying families.
  • Sell yearly, not monthly. Card fees take ~9% of $4.99 (2.9% + 30¢ ≈ $0.44) but only ~4% of $39.99. Tinybeans' members mostly hold annual plans 5.
  • Web, not app stores. We're a web app, so Stripe Checkout and no 15–30% Apple/Google cut.
  • Coins ≠ money. The Pass must never be bought with Coins, and Coins must never be sold. That keeps your Nov 8 rule and avoids contest/gambling issues.
  • Perks need real businesses. As with the Coffee Pass: plan on ~50 cafe visits to sign 20, and get the free item in writing.
  • Paid never changes rankings. Same rule as the Nov 6 plan.

6. How to test before building (cheap, ~1–2 days)

Test 1 · Roam Pass pre-sale

Sign 10 cafes first, then open a pre-sale page with Stripe Checkout for "Long Days 2027". Pass if 50 passes sell in 4 weeks ESTIMATE. Money is refunded if we don't reach 20 cafes.

Test 2 · Family+ fake door

A "Family+ · $39.99/yr · coming soon" card on Family Home and the passport page, with a "Notify me" button. PostHog counts views → taps → emails. Pass if 3%+ of those who see it tap ESTIMATE.

Test 3 · Yearbook demand

At the end of Long Nights (Mar 14, 2027), make each kid's free yearbook page. Offer a printed copy for $25. Pass if 5%+ of families with stamps order ESTIMATE.
